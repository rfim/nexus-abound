Why Lifepal's regulated insurance marketplace maps onto Abound's consumer lending.

In Lifepal, the other side was the insurers; at Abound, it's the loan itself. Lifepal connected people with the insurers that cover them, and priced and checked risk on the way; Abound connects people's bank data, through Open Banking, to a lending decision it makes and funds. In both, a transactional database is the system of record, data changes after the fact, a customer can ask to be forgotten, and a real financial decision depends on the number being right. The data problems that follow are the ones NEXUS was built to solve.
