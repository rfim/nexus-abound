Different product, same plumbing.

Why Lifepal's regulated insurance marketplace maps onto Abound's consumer lending platform.

Lifepal sold regulated insurance; Abound lends, using Open Banking data and AI to decide. Both run on a transactional database that the business, its reports and its risk models all depend on, and that nobody should query for analytics. In both, data changes after the fact, a customer can ask to be forgotten, and a real financial decision depends on the number being right. The problems that follow are the ones NEXUS was built to solve.
