Nexus, for Abound.

Every change in Aurora, in the lake exactly once. Proven against the source. Ready for model calibration.

NEXUS is the platform I built and ran at Lifepal, a regulated insurance marketplace: CDC from PostgreSQL and MySQL into an S3 lake with AWS DMS and Debezium, Spark and Glue merges that are idempotent under replay, and the feature pipelines behind risk and fraud models. Here it is applied to Abound's stack, Aurora → DMS → Glue → Iceberg → Athena: every change keyed on its change sequence, every delete a tombstone, every table reconciled to the source, and calibration data built as of the decision. Everything below runs at rfim.github.io/nexus-abound.
