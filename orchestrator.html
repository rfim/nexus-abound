    The same steps. On a schedule.

    On AWS the batches become an hourly Glue job, and the rarer steps become their own runs, chained by Step Functions.

    Hourly · Glue trigger

    cron(10 * * * ? *)

    Land new DMS files to bronze, apply to silver, run the quality rules. A failing rule fails the job.

    Nightly

    Reconcile, then gold

    Reconciliation against an Aurora read replica, history tables, the calibration set and the loan book.

    On failure

    Investigate → reload

    A Lambda posts the failing keys to on-call. The reload runs as a one-off Fargate task, then re-checks.

    Weekly

    Compact and expire

    rewrite_data_files, expire_snapshots and remove_orphan_files. Erasure requests are applied first.
