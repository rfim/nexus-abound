Aurora to the lake. One change at a time.

A change is committed in Aurora. DMS writes it to S3, a Glue job lands it in bronze and applies it to silver, and Athena and Data Science read the result. Pick one of the six problems from the run and watch it get handled.

Press Play.

    The file layout, column names and MERGE statements are the real ones from src/nexus/bronze.py and silver.py. The merge logic runs in your browser, ported line for line; the pipeline runs the same rules in Spark on Iceberg, and the tests check them.
